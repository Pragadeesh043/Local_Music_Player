# MP4A Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026.

MP4A is a local music player. It does not require an MP4A account and does not send your music library or usage analytics to the MP4A developer.

## Files and data on your PC

When you add files or choose folders to watch, MP4A reads those files to play and index your music. It stores file paths, track metadata, album art, playback history and analysis, and settings such as saved DSP presets on your device. It also keeps local diagnostic logs. MP4A uses these records to display your library, preserve your preferences, and report playback or indexing errors.

MP4A uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to render its bundled interface and runs bundled FFmpeg tools locally to read and analyze media files. Microsoft manages distribution and updates for Microsoft Store installs and the WebView2 runtime under its own privacy terms. If you open an external support link or contact us, the destination service handles that interaction under its own privacy terms.

## Your choices

You choose which files and folders MP4A can index. You can remove folders from the library. To clear MP4A's local index, caches, settings, and logs, close the app and clear its local Soundroom data folder. Clearing that folder resets the library and preferences. Your original music files remain in their existing locations.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use [MP4A support](https://github.com/Pragadeesh043/MP4A/issues). GitHub issues are public, so leave out private file paths, logs, and personal information.
